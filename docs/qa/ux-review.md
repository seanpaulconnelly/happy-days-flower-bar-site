## Summary

The page does the one job it has. On a 375×667 phone the business name, H1, lead and the primary CTA are all above the fold; the header Inquire button is visible in every one of 18 viewport frames from top to footer; one tap from anywhere lands the visitor on the Inquiry H2 with the first field already in view; price, guest range and what's included sit on one screen after a single tap on the secondary CTA. The form is the spec's nine fields in the spec's order, six required and marked, labels always visible, no placeholders, the right keyboard per field, native date and select pickers, validation messages verbatim from §6.4, focus moved to the first invalid field on submit, success and error panels per §6.3/D9 with the visitor's values kept and a prefilled `mailto:` fallback. The owner's four likes/dislikes are honoured: three nav items and no dropdown, an always-present clickable Inquire button instead of a buried form, edge-to-edge photography, and no unbroken text (tallest text-only run is the About copy at 350 px on 375, half a screen).

No blockers. One should-fix (premature required-field errors on blur of an untouched field) and four nits, all behaviour/layout, none touching copy.

## Mobile ergonomics (375, with 320 cross-check)

- **Thumb reach.** Header Inquire is right-aligned in the top-right zone (x 280–354 at 375; x 236–310 at 320). ux-spec §2.1 accepts this for a low-frequency single tap; no bottom bar or floating button, so nothing ever covers form fields.
- **Tap targets (probe, CSS px).** Wordmark 85×44; Flower Bar 69×44; About 38×44; header Inquire 73×36 + `::before` = 44 tall; hero CTA 226×44; secondary 246×45; band CTA 226×44; submit 284×44 (full width); every form control 48 px tall; FAQ summaries 332×93 (full row is the hit area); footer Instagram 69×44, Facebook 66×44, Email 38×44. Everything meets 44 px height; About and Email are 38 px wide with 16–24 px clear space on both sides, which passes WCAG 2.5.8 and is fine for a text link. The two text links inside the success/error panels are the exception — see UX-3.
- **Spacing between header links.** 16 px between Flower Bar / About / Inquire at 375; 8 px at 320 (`gap-2`, request Q8 accepted). Nothing wraps at 320; Inquire stays visible (`5e/320.png` top; probe `320-y00000.png`).
- **Select.** Native `<select>`, 48 px, custom chevron, "Choose one" as a disabled empty option so nothing is preselected; 8 options in the owner's order + Other. Native picker on iOS/Android — the owner's "too many items on the drop down" complaint was about nav, and the event-type list is short (`form/idle.png`, region "Type of Event").
- **Keyboard per field (probe).** Email `type=email inputmode=email autocomplete=email spellcheck=false autocapitalize=off`; Phone `type=tel inputmode=tel autocomplete=tel`; Guests `type=number inputmode=numeric min=1 step=1`; Name `autocomplete=name`; Business `autocomplete=organization`; Date/Location/Type/Notes `autocomplete=off` as specified. Control font size 16.08 px, so iOS does not zoom on focus.
- **Date input.** `type=date` with `min` = today's local date (`todayIso()` avoids the UTC off-by-one). Chromium shows `mm/dd/yyyy` with a calendar glyph (`form/idle.png`, region "Event Date"); iOS shows its wheel picker. A typed past date is caught by the JS rule ("Choose a date that hasn't passed yet.") on blur (probe `pastDate`).
- **Gallery** is edge-to-edge at 375 (images at x 0–184 and 192–375, 8 px gutter) (probe `375-y06000.png`; `5e/375.png` gallery region).
- **Sticky header** at all widths, opaque cream, hairline after 8 px of scroll (`Header.tsx`). Note for other reviewers: in the full-page captures `form/{error,submitting}.png` the header appears part-way down the image — that is the full-page-screenshot stitching a sticky element at the capture-time viewport position, not a layout bug; the viewport series shows it pinned at the top in every frame.

## Form friction

- **Field count and order** match the spec exactly (`Inquiry.tsx` `ORDER`; `form/idle.png`): Name*, Business / Organization, Email*, Phone, Event Date*, Event Location*, Type of Event*, [Tell us the type of event* when Other], Estimated Number of Guests*, Anything else we should know?. Labels verbatim; no placeholders; "Required fields are marked *" above the first field; asterisk is `aria-hidden` and the control carries `required` + `aria-required` (`Field.tsx`).
- **Validation timing.** JS validation with `noValidate`; each field validates on blur and again on submit; messages clear as soon as the field becomes valid; no field gains a message mid-typing (probe `phoneMidTyping` null). Messages are the §6.4 strings verbatim (`formCopy.ts`). One friction point: an untouched required field errors the moment it loses focus, e.g. tap Name, change your mind, tap Business → "Enter your name." appears (probe `blurUntypedName`). See UX-1.
- **Submit with errors.** Nothing is sent; the summary "Check the highlighted fields and try again." renders in the `role="alert"` region directly above the button; all six required-field errors show with icon + red border; focus moves to the first invalid control in DOM order (probe `emptySubmit`: active `inquiry-name`, 6 errors; `form/invalid.png`; probe `375-empty-submit2.png`). Everything typed is kept. On 375 the summary sits ≈ 1,100 px below the focused Name field, so a sighted visitor sees the inline error under Name rather than the summary — acceptable, the inline message is the actionable one and the alert is announced.
- **"Other" reveal.** Choosing Other reveals "Tell us the type of event *" directly beneath the select via `hidden` (out of the tab order when not in play), the box becomes required and receives focus (probe `otherReveal`: visible, required, active). Payload/mailto value is `Other: <text>`. Switching away hides the box again. A stale error can survive the switch — see UX-5.
- **Submitting.** Button disabled, label "Sending…", `aria-busy` on the form, status region announces "Sending…" (`form/submitting.png`). Double-submit guarded. Fields remain editable during the send — see UX-4.
- **Success (D9).** Form replaced by a mint panel inside the card; H2 and intro stay; heading "Thanks, Dana Whitfield!" and the approved auto-reply paragraph verbatim with the name substituted; "Send another inquiry" restores an empty form and focuses Name; focus moves to the panel heading (`form/success.png`, `form/nonce-blocked-success.png`). The "within 2 business days" expectation is on screen — the only time promise on the page, as §8.3 asked.
- **Error.** Form and every value intact; panel above the button with heading "We couldn't send your inquiry.", body, and "Or email us directly" — a `mailto:hello@…` with subject `Flower bar inquiry — {name}` and one label-per-line body in spec order, blanks omitted, capped at 1,500 characters (`mailto.ts`); button re-enabled with its original label; focus to the error heading (`form/error.png`). Never says "spam"; quarantined submissions show success.
- **Abandonment risks by persona.** Corporate planner: Business / Organization is second, so "you speak to businesses" is immediate; a flexible date is absorbed by the notes field (as §8.1 accepted). Individual host: only required fields are marked, so Business / Organization reads as skippable; "Shower or private celebration" and "Fundraiser" are in the list; guest count accepts any whole number ≥ 1 and rejects decimals with the "like 40" hint (probe `guestDecimal`). No budget field, no account, no upload. Nothing here would make either buyer abandon.

## Findings

No blockers.

### Should-fix

**UX-1 — Required-field errors fire on blur of a field the visitor never typed in.**
Evidence: probe `blurUntypedName` — focus Name, press Tab without typing → "Enter your name." appears under an empty field (same for Email, Event Date, Event Location, Type of Event, Guests). Code: `Inquiry.tsx` `validateOnBlur` runs the full rule set for the field on every blur. On a phone this is the "tap Name, then decide to read the intro / tap Business first" case; on a keyboard, tabbing through the form to see what it asks paints up to six red errors before a single character is typed. Premature validation is a documented abandonment driver (Baymard inline-validation research: errors before the user has had a chance to be right feel like scolding) and cuts against the owner's "Easy to use".
Change (behaviour only, no strings): keep blur validation, but on blur run only the _format_ rules (email shape, phone digits, past date, whole-number guests) and skip the `required` rules until the field has held a non-empty value at least once or a submit has been attempted (`showSummary`). Implementation sketch: track `dirty: Set<ValueKey>` (add on first non-empty `setValue`); in `validateOnBlur`, if the value is empty and the key is not dirty and `!showSummary`, clear that key's error instead of setting it. Submit behaviour is unchanged (all six required errors, summary, focus to first invalid).

### Nits

**UX-2 — CTA anchor jumps do not move focus to the Name field (ux-spec §2 deviation).**
Evidence: probe `headerInquireActive` = `BODY` after clicking header/hero/band Inquire; the next Tab does land on `#inquiry-name` (Chromium sequential-focus start point). On phones, force-focusing Name would raise the keyboard over the H2 and intro on the very screen the visitor just jumped to, which is worse than what ships (Name at 511 px, in view, one tap away). Recommendation: keep the current behaviour; amend ux-spec §2 at the next strategist pass to "rely on the browser's sequential-focus start point; do not autofocus on mobile". If the engineer wants parity with the spec for keyboard users only, `focus({ preventScroll: true })` the `#inquire` section (tabindex −1) on `hashchange`, not the input.

**UX-3 — The two panel text links are under the 44 px hit-area rule that every other control meets.**
Evidence: `form/success.png` "Send another inquiry" and `form/error.png` "Or email us directly" are inline text links ≈ 22 px tall (design-spec §2 token table: `min-h-tap` on "every link/button/summary hit area"). Change: add `inline-flex min-h-tap items-center` (and `-my-2` or equivalent so the panel rhythm does not grow) to both, `Inquiry.tsx` lines ≈ 319 and ≈ 569.

**UX-4 — Fields stay editable while "Sending…".**
Evidence: `form/submitting.png`; `Inquiry.tsx` sets `aria-busy` and disables the button only. ux-spec §6.3 asked for "fields stay visible and read-only" so a late edit cannot diverge from what was sent. Change: set `readOnly={submitting}` on the inputs/textarea and `disabled={submitting}` on the select (a `<fieldset disabled>` would also work but drops focus). Low impact — the send is short and double-submit is already guarded.

**UX-5 — A stale "Other" error can reappear.**
Evidence: probe `otherAfterSwitch` — choose Other, blur the empty box (error shown), switch to Fundraiser: the box hides but `errors.eventTypeOther` remains; choosing Other again reveals the box with "Tell us what type of event you're planning." already showing before the visitor has typed. Change: in the select's `onChange`, when the new value is not `Other`, clear `errors.eventTypeOther` (and `values.eventTypeOther` if you want the box empty on re-reveal).

### Observations (no change requested)

- Two green fills share the first viewport at 375 (header pill + hero CTA). Accepted by design review 5a-05 and ux-spec §2; the pill is visibly the smaller twin.
- FAQ closed list is 1.4 screens at 375×667 rather than "one phone screen" (ux-spec §1.2). The extra height buys 93 px rows; leave it.
- Success panel greets by name in both the heading and the first sentence of the body — that is D9's chosen option and the body is locked copy.
- Full-page captures `form/{error,submitting}.png` show the header mid-image: capture artifact of a sticky element, not a bug.
- Price is ≈ 5.3 screens down on a 375×667 phone (≈ 4.3 on 375×812); ux-spec §8.1 estimated "screens 4–5". The secondary CTA at 1.7 screens is the mitigation and it works (one tap → price, range and included on one screen). Do not add anything between Hero and Packages.
